# SGS state generation plan

**Goal:** Build physically valid, varied starts for all five assembly tasks, with the unused arm nearby and available to help.

**Status:** Pilot v2 is complete: 50/50 states across all 25 groups, 60/60 production/fine-step receipts, 31/31 strict preflight and 13/13 CUDA checks. The bounded pilot training experiment is active before scaling to the full 8,000-state target. Accepted v14 physics and the 0.5 mm limit remain unchanged. [Evidence and live status](docs/RUN_READINESS.md).

1. **Freeze the qualified environment.** Use the shared training/Quest model and record physics, CAD, native-library and validator identities. Replay historical proposals against that identity before accepting them.
2. **Cover all 25 task/family combinations.** Motor, horn, screw, retainer and yoke each get loose, pickup, in-hand, near and partial starts. Prerequisites are already assembled and remain dynamic. Partial means engaged but unfinished; near means outside engagement. Preserve screw partial starts supported by their actual threads.
3. **Place the other arm nearby.** Use several open-gripper ready poses on its own side of the receiving assembly, outside the active arm's approach corridor. Choose offsets using actual geometry and reachable IK; check every arm link, fingers, table and parts. Start with up to ±10 mm position variation inside this clear region. The arm initially holds nothing, and both arms remain policy-controlled. In loose starts, place both arms in clear ready poses.
4. **Vary coherent physical poses.** Start with loose-scene/part variation of ±8 mm XY and ±8° yaw; use tighter pickup/mating bounds from the existing generator. Vary grasps and insertion depths, then add small perturbations within actual clearances. Move assembled predecessors together and observation points with their bodies. Reject placements requiring large unplanned assembly rotations/translations to solve IK. Record nominal layout separately from noise; keep the selected 448-point input.
5. **Qualify and inspect a pilot.** Generate one TRAIN and one independent EVAL start per combination: 50 accepted states. Check contacts/material overlap, loaded grasp retention, prerequisite retention, nonterminal reward, arm clearance and bounded-action replay. Replay every partial at the finer timestep too. Show all families, report failures, actual pose ranges and accepted states/hour. Prepared snapshots do not prove a reachable assembly sequence.
6. **Scale only passing distributions.** Target 256 TRAIN + 64 EVAL per combination: **6,400 TRAIN + 1,600 EVAL**. Preserve disjoint seeds, reject duplicates, checkpoint progress and publish only a complete current-identity bank. Keep per-entry SGS unchanged; evaluate subtask-loose and full-sequence starts separately. Measure training throughput before scheduling a long run.

**Done when:** All 25 groups meet their counts and existing physical gates, nearby-arm starts pass replay, and coverage/timing reports accompany the bank. Training remains a separate step.
