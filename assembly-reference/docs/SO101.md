# Dual SO101 / Hiwonder assembly

Goal: adapt AssembleNIST to two clamped SO101 helper arms assembling a loose Hiwonder base segment, with physical contacts and an explicitly evaluated learning curriculum. **Full assembly from loose parts is not solved.** Scripted motion, prepared reset states and guided mechanical checks are separate evidence from learned assembly.

## Scope and source

This fork starts at [upstream commit `eb5d15e004591d380c0d521da67e025ebee37703`](https://github.com/suniique/assembly-nist/tree/eb5d15e004591d380c0d521da67e025ebee37703), without earlier experimental policy weights. The five task episodes are `motor`, `horn`, `screw`, `retainer`, and `yoke`, in that order. They cover the base segment of the [assembly video, 0:27–5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-pcWH1APvew&t=27). The top horn and its center screw are included; bottom horn, wiring and other assembly screws are excluded.

Each episode initializes its predecessor parts at settled reference poses. All six target parts and the screwdriver remain free bodies; there are no attachment constraints. This task decomposition does not demonstrate a complete sequential assembly policy. Loose parts vary independently by ±8 mm in table position and ±8° yaw. The current helper bases sit 0.5 m apart, at X = ±0.25 m, facing inward; clamps and the table are static. Full task reachability at this layout remains under investigation.

The source SimBa networks, PPO update and SGS sampler implementation are retained. Adapter changes provide twelve continuous relative position actions (five arm joints plus one gripper per arm), 320 sampled geometry points, and five frames of 53 proprioceptive values. Control is 25 Hz; native MuJoCo physics steps at 0.5 ms. Episodes last at most 15 seconds. Binary success requires all relevant part keypoints within 1 mm, the active part released from both robots and tool, and stable retention. Current runs select eleven eligible poses spanning 0.4 seconds; older checkpoints retain their ten-sample velocity criterion. This control-sampled reward is separate from fine-step contact audits. The screw also requires thread and head-bearing contacts. Velocity, contact-depth and numerical guards reject invalid transitions before PPO.

## Geometry and assumptions

| Component | Source and qualification |
| --- | --- |
| Hiwonder target | Official [technical files](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1sAwusnQrOkSJRaRBbTIlIXnjiwk_TyVz), linked from the [Hiwonder manual](https://docs.hiwonder.com/projects/LeRobot/en/latest/docs/SO_ARM101_Open_Source_6_Axis_Robotic_Arm_User_Manual.html). Import `follower arm black.3dm` without material cuts; the importer verifies its SHA-256. |
| Helper arms | [TheRobotStudio SO101](https://github.com/TheRobotStudio/SO-ARM100/tree/a758567c3978dfeefe282ede0500085a48fe8f78/Simulation/SO101), pinned at `a758567c3978dfeefe282ede0500085a48fe8f78`, as a model for the [WowRobo kit](https://shop.wowrobo.com/products/so-arm101-diy-kit-assembled-version-1?variant=46588641607897). Actual helper calibration is unverified. |
| Contacts | Target surface SDF grids preserve cavities; helper jaw SDFs sample the untouched STL at 0.2 mm. Grid interpolation approximates surfaces. A thread-local grid-pointer cache changes lookup contention, not upstream SDF equations. |
| Horn screw | Hiwonder's [kit PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1W6aCQra-WSqgec-vhruyVK-N6hL8OcJT/view), page 6, lists an M3 × 5 × 7 washer-head self-tapping screw. The CAD screw and bore disagree. Simulation uses a nominal 3 mm × 5 mm screw, 7 mm flange, 0.5 mm helical pitch and 60 µm radial clearance. Pitch/profile are engineering assumptions, not measured hardware. |
| Reference poses | Horn and yoke reference poses move upward 1.1 mm to resolve vendor assembled-pose interference; their meshes remain unchanged. |
| Screwdriver | Nominal HOTO-style tool with a passive sleeve and spring-loaded saddle: closing the gripper physically presses the native button. No electrical action channel or grasp weld. See [design, references and failed robustness checks](SO101_TOOL.md). |

Masses, bounding-box inertia estimates, friction and actuator settings require hardware identification. The conservative 0.35 N m gripper cap is an assumption. Other arm limits are inherited from the source model and are not a calibrated WowRobo controller. Changing tool orientation, screw engagement and resistance to reaction torque remain open; an aligned button test does not qualify them.

The [motor/base fit audit](SO101_FIT.md) finds about 0.2 mm interference in untouched vendor triangles: a 31.8 mm rail cavity around a 32.0 mm motor. This is distinct from grid interpolation and from MuJoCo's reported contact distance. Printed-part dimensions and compliance remain unmeasured; neither exact hardware fit nor geometric nonintersection is established by the current soft-contact simulation.

One nominal full-arm tool pickup/press/stop/return now passes the native trajectory check; see [its scope and remaining grasp drift](SO101_TOOL.md). General pickup reliability, orientation changes and screw engagement remain open.

## Local setup

Commands run from the fork root. Tested with Python 3.12, MuJoCo 3.17.0, PyTorch 2.11/CUDA 12.8 on Linux. A C++17 compiler is required; install a CUDA-capable PyTorch build for your GPU before training. The optional asset tools need additional host memory during SDF generation.

```bash
python3 -m venv .venv
.venv/bin/python -m pip install -e '.[so101]'
```

Download the official `follower arm black.3dm` as `assets_local/hiwonder/follower.3dm`. Expected SHA-256: `f675f6049f94206c7b74ab73c40485913916babff1a6f4fc145dc9040672a2f8`. Vendor and derived assets stay local because their redistribution terms have not been established. The importer downloads the pinned public helper assets.

```bash
.venv/bin/python scripts/so101_assets.py
.venv/bin/python scripts/so101_sdf.py
.venv/bin/python scripts/so101_gripper_sdf.py
bash scripts/so101_build_plugins.sh
.venv/bin/python -m assembly_policy.so101.resets --count 32 --workers 2
.venv/bin/python scripts/so101_thread_check.py
.venv/bin/python scripts/so101_preflight.py --max-contact-depth .0004 --stability pose
```

The scene loader builds XML in memory and loads both compiled plugins. Preflight checks asset provenance, source preservation, task resets, observations/actions, unsuccessful loose starts, timeouts and the guided thread/button mechanisms. It binds its report to the model and exact bank files/manifest; it is not a full manipulation feasibility test. Repeat it after changing model assets or reset banks, using new bank and run directories for curriculum changes.

By default, reset generation creates independent loose train/evaluation seeds for all five tasks. Optional repeated `--trajectory PATH` arguments add task-specific pickup, held, partial and near states only when current-model recorded contacts qualify, including loaded contact on both jaws. `assembly_policy.so101.prepared` generates explicit seated-and-held motor starts; `prepared_motor_approach` and `prepared_parts` cover physical withdrawal and horn/retainer holds. These are prepared curriculum states, not successful loose assembly trajectories. Use `resets --extend-from OLD_BANK --out NEW_BANK --trajectory TRACE` to copy a bank and add coverage; nonempty output directories are rejected. Available families and provenance are recorded in the manifest. Independent evaluation starts are preserved.

The interactive viewer requires a display; `--assembled` initializes a reference pose. The default demo attempts scripted motor pickup, transport and release, with a penetration guard. ## Run and monitor learning

Native CPU physics remains the default and independent evaluation reference. The bounded experiments use the optional [whole-helper SDF GPU backend](SO101_GPU_CANDIDATE.md) after geometry, tool, environment and actual PPO checks pass. Its explicit tolerance is 1e-6, following MJWarp's float32 importer floor; native stays at 1e-8. Earlier stricter-tolerance failures are preserved. At 128 worlds, the initial pilot measures 61–67 complete learner transitions/s; later threaded endpoint forwarding reaches a 93.61 median in the final experiment. All runs are now stopped; [status and behavioral failures](SO101_STATUS.md) remain distinct from throughput.

For a bounded run after a passing preflight, choose a future UTC end time:

```bash
SO101_END_UTC='2026-11-06T06:00:00+00:00'  # replace with the intended future boundary
.venv/bin/python scripts/so101_overnight.py \
  --run-dir runs/so101_pilot --end-utc "$SO101_END_UTC" \
  --bank-dir data/so101/banks --envs 64 --threads 16 --steps 32 \
  --max-contact-depth .0004 --stability pose
```

The supervisor retains source PPO settings and scales the SGS per-entry floor to `1.6 / number_of_training_entries` for the small pilot bank. This is a documented sampler-parameter adaptation, not a change to SGS equations. Run it in a persistent terminal/session. It writes `supervisor.json`, `health.json`, `health.jsonl`, `log.jsonl`, trainer logs and checkpoints; default reviews every 7,200 seconds snapshot health/checkpoints and evaluate two independent loose starts per task. These small reviews are diagnostics, not a success-rate benchmark.

Numerical failures stop for review; unexpected trainer exits allow at most two checkpoint restarts. Memory/disk and stalled-iteration guards also stop the run. To request a checkpointed stop:

```bash
touch runs/so101_pilot/STOP
```

The trainer finishes its current iteration and saves; inspect `supervisor.json` for `stopped` or `needs_review`. An existing `STOP` prevents restart. Same-run checkpoint resume requires matching model, bank, task order, environment and physics runtime. Source checkpoints restore weights, optimizer, sampler and counters; live environments and RNG are reinitialized, so this is not exact trajectory continuation. Reviews use new numbered directories when a supervisor resumes. For an already generated and validated versioned bank, use a separate preflight report and run directory:

```bash
.venv/bin/python scripts/so101_preflight.py \
  --bank-dir data/so101/banks_v2 --out artifacts_local/preflight_v2.json \
  --max-contact-depth .0004 --stability pose
.venv/bin/python scripts/so101_overnight.py \
  --run-dir runs/so101_v2 --end-utc "$SO101_END_UTC" \
  --bank-dir data/so101/banks_v2 --preflight artifacts_local/preflight_v2.json \
  --init-policy runs/so101_pilot/checkpoint.pt \
  --max-contact-depth .0004 --stability pose
```

`--init-policy` requires matching canonical assets and task order, loads compatible weights, resets optimizer/SGS, and records the parent checkpoint hash/backend. A separately qualified backend may be selected in a new run; same-run resume rejects runtime changes. Stop the previous resource-intensive run before launching its replacement.

For the qualified local GPU archive, preflight and launch explicitly select the same runtime:

```bash

.venv/bin/python scripts/so101_overnight.py \
  --run-dir runs/so101_gpu_new --end-utc "$SO101_END_UTC" \

```

Add `--init-policy PATH/checkpoint.pt` only for an explicitly intended weights-only continuation. Recreate local archives using the GPU qualification document; an old preflight is rejected after runtime changes.

After the 14:01 loose-policy safety regression, a separate experiment opts into
`--safety-penalty 1` on both preflight and supervisor (`--so101-safety-penalty 1`
for the trainer). This subtracts one only on a safety terminal; legacy default
is zero. Paired recorded-action tests preserve physics, observations and episode
outcomes exactly. Learning benefit is an evaluation question, not a consequence
of that semantic test. Current GPU endpoint forwarding uses eight CPU workers;
the shared model is read-only and each worker owns distinct native data. Exact
frozen and live carryover/reset checks pass. Both changes require a fresh run and
matching preflight; see [current results](SO101_STATUS.md).

The explicit `pose` mode requires eleven eligible control samples spanning 0.4 s, with world-pose drift at most 0.25 mm and 0.01 rad from the window's first pose. This avoids rejecting motionless assemblies solely for high-frequency contact-velocity jitter. The optional depth monitor reads the [native contact sensor](https://mujoco.readthedocs.io/en/3.17.0/XMLreference.html#sensor-contact) every physics step and checks the final integrated pose; excessive depth terminates the episode after that control block. It detects discrete contact violations, not continuous geometric nonintersection. Sensor instrumentation leaves canonical physics/bank states unchanged. Older checkpoints retain their recorded velocity-based rule. Reproduce qualification with `scripts/check_so101_contact_guard.py` and `scripts/check_so101_task_completion.py` after generating their referenced physical traces; the latter includes held, released, loose and falling cases.

Evaluate a saved checkpoint against its matching bank:

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m assembly_policy.so101.evaluate \
  --policy runs/so101_pilot/checkpoint.pt --bank-dir data/so101/banks \
  --trials 32 --nthread 4 --out runs/so101_pilot/evaluation.json
```

Judge progress by independent task successes, reset-family coverage, safety/timeout outcomes and physical traces alongside finite PPO statistics, throughput and resource headroom. Falling critic loss can occur while every assembly attempt fails. `assets_local/`, `artifacts_local/`, `data/` and `runs/` are ignored; reports/checkpoints are local evidence and are not included with the source fork. No hardware transfer or complete assembly success is claimed.

The fixed prepared-near holdout (`scripts/check_so101_near_holdout.py`) tests four unseen holds each for motor, horn and retainer. The frozen v2 iteration-35 policy passes all twelve mean-action and twelve sampled-action first episodes on native physics. This measures release/retention from prepared holds, not pickup or insertion. One of the twenty-four success windows contains two brief fine-step finger contacts despite satisfying the production control-sampled release criterion. Independent loose-table checks remain 0/10; evidence is under `artifacts_local/near_holdout/`.
